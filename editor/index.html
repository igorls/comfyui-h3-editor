    Rate a range

    This sends that part of the cut, at half size, to Google's Gemini with your own API key. It is deleted there once it is rated. Each criterion comes back as a level, with how sure the model is.

    Writing with a language model

    Rewrite and Write shots send text to the model you choose: the project's look and language, the cast's names and descriptions, and the clips' text. No pictures and no sound. A model run by this ComfyUI keeps it on this machine; any other provider is called from this browser. These settings, any key included, stay in this browser and are never saved in a project.

    Keyboard shortcuts

    Space
    Play or pause
    J, K, L
    Back one second, pause, play
    Left, Right
    One frame. Hold Shift for one second
    Up, Down
    Previous or next edit
    Home, End
    Start or end of the scene
    S
    Split at the playhead
    Esc
    Select nothing
    Tab, Enter
    Reach a clip or a sound on the timeline, and select it
    Delete
    On the timeline, remove a clip from it, or close a selected gap. In the Clips panel, delete the clip or bin
    Shift+Delete
    Remove a clip from the timeline and close the space
    I and O
    Mark the start and the end of a range at the playhead, for Rate. Shift-drag on the ruler marks one too; Esc clears it
    [ and ]
    Flip between takes of the selected clip
    , and .
    Slip the selected clip: show an earlier or a later part of its take, without moving it. With Shift, half a second
    Alt+drag
    Slip a clip on the timeline
    Ctrl+wheel
    Zoom the timeline at the pointer
    \
    Fit the whole scene
    Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Shift+Z
    Undo, redo
    ?
    Show this list
