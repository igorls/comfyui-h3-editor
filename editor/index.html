      Keyboard shortcuts

      Space
      Play or pause
      J, K, L
      Back one second, pause, play
      Left, Right
      One frame. Hold Shift for one second
      Up, Down
      Previous or next edit
      Home, End
      Start or end of the scene
      S
      Split at the playhead
      N
      Write a note on the frame in the viewer: pin, box or draw on it. With a range marked by I and O, the note covers the range
      Esc
      Select nothing
      Tab, Enter
      Reach a clip or a sound on the timeline, and select it
      Shift+F10
      Open actions for the focused item. Arrow keys move through actions and Escape returns to the item.
      Delete
      On the timeline, remove a clip from it, or close a selected gap. In the Clips panel, delete the clip or bin
      Shift+Delete
      Remove a clip from the timeline and close the space
      I and O
      Mark In at the playhead and Out after that frame. Loop repeats this range. Shift-drag on the ruler marks a range too; Esc clears it
      [ and ]
      Flip between takes of the selected clip
      , and .
      Slip the selected clip: show an earlier or a later part of its take, without moving it. With Shift, half a second
      Alt+drag
      Slip a clip on the timeline
      Ctrl+wheel
      Zoom the timeline at the pointer
      \
      Fit the whole scene
      Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Shift+Z
      Undo, redo
      ?
      Show this list
